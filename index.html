OUR MISSION

One community. Every stage of the healthcare journey.

Detroit Youth Health Expo (DYHE) is dedicated to exposing high school students to diverse healthcare careers while helping them build meaningful connections with healthcare professionals.

Through monthly programs, educational sessions, panels, networking events, mentorship, and career exploration activities, students gain insight into different healthcare pathways and the academic and professional steps needed to pursue them.

DYHE also provides undergraduate students with opportunities to mentor and support high school participants as near-peer mentors, facilitators, and role models. Undergraduate participants can engage in year-round programming designed for pre-medical students, including professional development, networking, leadership, mentorship, and engagement with healthcare professionals.
