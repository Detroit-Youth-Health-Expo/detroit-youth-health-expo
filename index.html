OUR MISSION

One community. Every stage of the healthcare journey.

Detroit Youth Health Expo (DYHE) is dedicated to exposing high school students to diverse healthcare careers while helping them build meaningful connections with healthcare professionals.

Through monthly programs, educational sessions, panels, networking, mentorship, and career exploration activities, students gain insight into different healthcare pathways and the academic and professional steps needed to pursue them.

DYHE also creates opportunities for undergraduate students to serve as near-peer mentors, facilitators, and role models, while offering year-round programming for pre-medical students.
